JOHN 3 • BORN AGAIN • NEW LIFE

I GIVE MY LIFE
To Jesus.

"For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life." — John 3:16 (KJV)

Just like Nicodemus in John 3 — salvation is not religion. It is being Born Again. If you believe in your heart that Jesus died for you and rose again, you are saved today.

PRAY THIS OUT LOUD — THE SINNER'S PRAYER

Lord Jesus, I believe You are the Son of God. I believe You died on the cross for my sins and rose again on the third day.

I confess I am a sinner. I turn away from my old life today. I open my heart to You. Come into my life. Be my Lord. Be my Saviour. Wash me clean by Your blood.

I receive Your forgiveness. I receive eternal life. I am Born Again. I am a child of God. I will follow You all the days of my life.

In Jesus' Name, Amen.
