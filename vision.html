01 — THE CALL

Catch Fire.
Rise As A Flame.

A Generational Response to a Divine Assignment.

FLAME Prayer Mantle is a call to consecration, alignment and collective responsibility.

We believe there is a generational mandate that requires more than the preservation of spiritual language, the continuation of religious activity or the celebration of ministry performance.

It calls for a people who understand the demands of their assignments, recognise the times, position themselves in truth and become visible expressions of the light they have received.

We are gathering around this burden to understand the present spiritual condition, align ourselves with the purposes of God and translate revelation into faithful implementation.

This is more than an event.
It is a generational response to a divine assignment.

A spark does not need to look like an inferno to carry the beginning of a great fire.

02 — THE NEW CRUSE

A New Cruse.
A Prepared Vessel.

2 Kings 2:20–22

“And he said, Bring me a new cruse, and put salt therein. And they brought it to him.”

The new cruse presents a picture of a prepared vessel and a new season of responsibility.

We are not merely seeking a new gathering, a new name or a new expression. We are concerned with the condition of the vessel.

The assignment requires consecration. The work must be approached with purity, surrender, spiritual understanding and genuine communion with God.

The emphasis is not novelty for its own sake, but readiness for responsibility.

03 — THE MANTLE AND THE GENERATIONAL ASSIGNMENT

The Mantle Is Bigger
Than One Individual.

1 Kings 19:15–16 • 1 Kings 19:19–21 • 2 Kings 2:13–14

The prophetic account of Elijah and Elisha reminds us that divine assignments extend beyond individual lifetimes.

The Lord instructed Elijah concerning other assignments, including the anointing of Jehu. The responsibility was never intended to terminate with one individual.

The mantle therefore speaks to continuity, stewardship and responsibility.

FLAME is not an attempt to replace established ministries, honour personalities or compete with previous generations. We recognise the spiritual labour, sacrifice and inheritance of those who have gone before us.

Our burden is to receive responsibility, understand our times and faithfully contribute to what must become visible in our generation.

WE ARE NOT ARISING TO REPLACE.
WE ARE ARISING TO BRIDGE.

To bridge the gap between revelation and implementation.
Between spiritual inheritance and generational responsibility.
Between knowing the truth and embodying the truth.
Between receiving the light and becoming its visible expression.

04 — THE GAP WE ARE RESPONDING TO

We See Jesus Crowned.
We Must Understand
Our Responsibility.

Hebrews 2:8–9

Scripture declares that all things have been placed under the authority of our Lord, yet it acknowledges that we do not presently see all things subjected to Him.

We see Jesus crowned with glory and honour. This creates a responsibility for the Church and for every generation entrusted with the truth.

FLAME responds to the gap between what has been accomplished in Christ and what must become visible in our lives, communities and world.

We are not arising to establish another authority in place of Christ. We are arising in response to His lordship, seeking to embody His truth, demonstrate His light and faithfully execute the responsibilities entrusted to our generation.

05 — THE FOUNDATION OF CONSECRATION

Every Assignment Has
A Corresponding Demand
For Consecration.

The condition of the vessel matters as much as the assignment it carries.

1. Holiness and Purity

A generation set apart unto God, committed to clean hands, pure hearts and lives that reflect His nature.

2. Total Surrender

A people who understand that the assignment belongs to God and that every area of life must remain submitted to Him.

3. Uninterrupted Communion

Prayer is not an event we occasionally attend. It is a life of continuous fellowship and dependence upon God.

4. Spiritual Alignment

Understanding the times, recognising the demands of our assignments and positioning ourselves according to the Word.

Leviticus 6:13 • Romans 12:1–2 • Matthew 5:8 • 1 Thessalonians 5:17

FLAME is not built around religious performance, outward appearances or ministerial titles. It is about genuine transformation.

06 — THE GENERATIONAL MANDATE

A Generation That
Does Not Just Receive
The Light.

A Generation That
Becomes The Light.

Matthew 5:14–16

We are carrying a burden for a generation that will not merely inherit spiritual language but will embody spiritual reality.

A generation that understands holiness, practises truth, demonstrates compassion, carries responsibility and faithfully represents Christ.

We have gathered believers for many years. We recognise the importance of strengthening the household of faith. However, we also recognise a responsibility that extends beyond gathering believers.

It is time to intentionally reach those who have not yet encountered the truth. We desire to see a generation that carries the light into communities, families, institutions and the lives of those who have not yet come to faith.

FLAME therefore embraces both the strengthening of believers and the reaching of non-believers. This is not merely about gathering people into a room. It is about carrying the message beyond the room.

FROM GATHERING THE BELIEVERS
TO REACHING THE WORLD.

08 — THE IMPLEMENTATION FRAMEWORK

Understand.
Position.
Implement.

01 — Understand The Status Quo

Recognise the spiritual condition of our generation, identify the gaps and understand the responsibilities before us.

02 — Position Ourselves

Return to the foundations of holiness, purity, surrender, communion and alignment with the Word.

03 — Implement With The Light

Translate revelation into action through prayer, teaching, worship, evangelism, service and practical expressions of the Kingdom.

09 — THE MINISTERIAL INVITATION

Whatever You Carry,
Our Generation
Needs It.

An invitation to ministers, Kingdom builders and spiritual labourers.

We recognise that every minister carries a measure of grace, experience, revelation, wisdom and responsibility.

We are therefore extending this invitation with an understanding that the assignment before us cannot be approached as the responsibility of one individual.

We are reaching out to ministers whose contributions, spiritual maturity, counsel and involvement can help us steward this generational mandate.

We are not approaching you merely because of your title or public recognition. We are approaching you because we recognise that what you carry has a place in the responsibility of this generation.

This is not an invitation to honour a personality. It is an invitation to honour an assignment.

What We Are Inviting You To Contribute

A. Spiritual and Administrative Partnership

Your presence. Your administration. Your counsel. Your ministry involvement. Your spiritual contribution. Your participation in the stewardship and execution of the assignment.

B. Financial and Material Partnership

We are working towards a budget to facilitate the gathering and its execution. We welcome financial contributions, resources, materials and practical support according to each partner's capacity and willingness.

Financial support is welcomed but does not determine the value of a minister's participation.

Possible Areas of Ministerial Involvement

• Prayer and intercession • Teaching and biblical instruction • Worship and spiritual ministry • Evangelism and outreach • Ministerial counsel and administration • Mobilisation and ministry connections • Media and communication • Practical and financial support

WE DO NOT NEED EVERYONE TO CARRY THE SAME THING.
WE NEED EVERYONE TO FAITHFULLY CONTRIBUTE WHAT THEY HAVE BEEN ENTRUSTED WITH.
