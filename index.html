01 — THE CENTRAL MESSAGE

THE CALL TO
CONSECRATION

This is a call to consecration, purity, submission, and total surrender to God. An invitation to believers to embrace a life of complete spiritual communion with God — recognizing that every discipline, teaching, and activity we have received is intended to sustain the fire of revival.

01

Consecration & Purity

Set apart. Clean hands, pure heart. A life that can carry holy fire.

02

Total Surrender

Not part of you. All of you. Submission to God in every area.

03

Uninterrupted Communion

Prayer is not an event. It is the place we live.

02 — THE PURPOSE OF COMMUNION

NOT ROUTINES.
INSTRUMENTS OF FIRE.

Prayer, the study of God's Word, worship, fellowship, obedience — these are not merely religious routines. They are instruments through which we cultivate, sustain, and continually fuel the fire of revival.

What we receive in the presence of God is not meant to end with personal encounters. It must produce a transformed life, spiritual maturity, and an ever-increasing hunger for God.

03 — THE VISION OF AN AWAKENING

REVIVAL BEGINS
WITHIN.

🔥 First In Hearts

We carry a greater expectation that the Lord Jesus Christ will release a divine decree, and in His appointed time, revival will erupt. It will first take place in the hearts of men and women who burn with genuine burden, hunger, and thirst for God and His Word. It begins within before it becomes visible without.

✨ Then In The World

As believers become filled with the Word and the Spirit, they will rise beyond personal experiences to become visible expressions of His light. Revival is not merely an atmosphere — it is a transformation that produces people who carry His presence, reflect His nature, proclaim His Word, and illuminate their generation.

📖 Hunger For The Word

Hunger and passion for God's Word. Being filled with the Holy Spirit. Raising a generation that becomes the light of the world.

MATTHEW 5:14 (KJV)

"Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid."

This scripture establishes the ultimate responsibility of those who have encountered the light of Christ: they must become light bearers in their generation.

04 — THE PROPHETIC EXPRESSION

AFTER WE CATCH FIRE,
WE RISE AS A FLAME.

Fire represents the awakening, encounter, and spiritual ignition that takes place within us through communion with God.

A Flame represents the visible expression of that fire. It illuminates, gives direction, and makes its presence known.

We are not called merely to experience revival. We are called to become its living expression. The objective is not simply to burn privately but to rise as a generation whose lives illuminate the world.
