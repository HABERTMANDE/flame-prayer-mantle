DISCONNECTED
FROM THE HEAD

The Doctrine of Religiosity - By Habert Mande Murunga

What if the greatest danger to the Church is not abandoning religion, but practising it while becoming disconnected from Christ?

Discover the truth behind Disconnected from the Head, and why returning to Christ is the beginning of genuine transformation.

What happens when we retain the language, practices, and appearance of Christianity, but lose sight of the life and purpose behind them?

In Disconnected from the Head, Habert Mande Murunga confronts one of the most subtle dangers in the Christian faith: the rise of religious activity without genuine spiritual formation.

Through biblical teaching and practical examination, this book challenges us to reconsider the relationship between doctrine and character, faith and conduct, prayer and obedience, spiritual authority and accountability. It explores how good practices can become empty routines, how religious identity can overshadow Christlike character, and how familiar explanations can sometimes keep us from taking responsibility.

Moving from God's original design, through the emergence and consequences of religiosity, to the call for restoration, this book invites believers, ministers, and church leaders to examine what they have normalized and return to the truth of Scripture.

This is not a call to abandon the Church, doctrine, or spiritual disciplines. It is a call to restore them to their rightful place under Christ.

Because the body was never designed to function independently of its Head.

The call is simple: Return to Christ. Let the truth we preach become the life we live.
